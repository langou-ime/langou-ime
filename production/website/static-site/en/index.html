🐾 FAQ

Does Langou read my chat history?
Langou only captures visible text from the current screen (up to 10 messages) to generate AI suggestions. Texts are encrypted in transit. No raw screenshots stored. All data processed in memory and released immediately. PIPL & GDPR compliant.
How many AI replies can I use per day?
All features are completely free with no limits! AI replies, smart input, cloud sync — everything is unlimited and always will be.
Which chat apps are supported?
WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Discord, WeChat, QQ, DingTalk, Lark, Slack and all major chat apps. Also supports prompt optimization for ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude.
How good are the AI replies?
Powered by MiMo-V2.5-Pro (420B parameters) with over 70% user adoption rate. AI considers context, emotion, and relationship to generate appropriate replies.
