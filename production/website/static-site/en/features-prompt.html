💡 Prompt Optimizer

Better prompts, better AI responses

Why Prompt Optimization Matters

Large language models are incredibly powerful — but only as powerful as the prompts you give them. A vague prompt like "write an email" produces mediocre results. A well-structured prompt with role definition, context, constraints, and output format produces exceptional results. Langou's Prompt Optimizer bridges this gap automatically.

🔬

How It Works

The Prompt Optimizer is a specialized language model trained to improve other prompts. When you type a rough prompt in any supported AI tool (ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini), Langou detects the context and offers optimization alternatives:

  1. Analysis: Your original prompt is analyzed for vagueness, missing constraints, unclear outputs, and ambiguous instructions
  2. Enhancement: The optimizer adds role definitions, output format specifications, boundary conditions, and explicit constraints
  3. Two Versions: You get a "Detailed" version (comprehensive, ideal for complex tasks) and a "Concise" version (streamlined, perfect for quick queries)
  4. Model-specific: Optimizations are tailored to the target model — what works best for ChatGPT may differ from DeepSeek or Claude
📝

Example: Before and After

Original prompt: "Write an email to my team about the project delay"

Optimized (Detailed): "Write a professional email to my software engineering team of 8 people about a 2-week delay in the Q3 mobile app release. The delay is due to unexpected database migration complexity. Include: (1) acknowledgment of the delay, (2) revised timeline, (3) specific action items for each team member. Keep tone transparent and constructive. Avoid blame. Include a brief Q&A section addressing likely concerns. Format: formal email with subject line, greeting, body sections, and signature."

Optimized (Concise): "Draft a brief team email about the 2-week app release delay. Transparent tone, no blame. Include: timeline change, action items, next steps. Sign as [Your Name]."

🎯

Model-Specific Optimization

Different AI models have different strengths and weaknesses. Our optimizer knows them:

  • ChatGPT-optimized: Adds system-like instructions, explicit reasoning steps, and constraints that GPT-4o handles well
  • DeepSeek-optimized: Leverages DeepSeek's strong reasoning capabilities with structured, chain-of-thought prompts
  • Claude-optimized: Uses Claude's strength at following detailed instructions with clear XML-like structure and safety framing
  • Gemini-optimized: Tailored for Gemini's multimodal capabilities and Google ecosystem

Common Use Cases

  • Email Writing: "Reply to client about budget proposal" → professional, structured business email
  • Coding Help: "Fix my Python function" → detailed debugging request with error context
  • Content Creation: "Write a blog post about AI" → specific outline, tone, audience, and length
  • Data Analysis: "Analyze this CSV" → precise instructions with column descriptions and output format
  • Translation: "Translate this to French" → context-aware translation with style notes
