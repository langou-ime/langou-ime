🎧 Customer Service Scenarios

Deliver exceptional support with the right tone

Service Excellence Through AI

Customer service is one of the most demanding communication contexts. You need to solve problems efficiently while making every customer feel heard and valued. Langou's Professional and Warm styles help customer service representatives maintain composure, empathy, and clarity — even in high-pressure situations. The Concise style helps with internal notes and quick updates.

🎧 Scenario 1: Handling a Billing Complaint

Context: Customer writes: "I was charged TWICE for my order. I want this fixed NOW."

Professional Style: "I sincerely apologize for this billing error. I completely understand how frustrating it is to see an unexpected charge. Let me resolve this for you right away: I've initiated a refund for the duplicate charge, which should appear in your account within 3-5 business days. I've also flagged your account to prevent this from happening again. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

Warm Style: "I'm so sorry this happened! Thank you for bringing it to our attention — that's definitely not the experience we want you to have. I've just processed a full refund for the duplicate charge. It'll be back in your account in a few days. I really appreciate your patience, and we're taking steps to make sure this doesn't happen again. ❤️"

🎧 Scenario 2: Product Inquiry

Context: Customer asks: "Does your app support voice input in Chinese?"

Professional Style: "Yes! Langou Input Method fully supports voice input in Chinese (Mandarin). It's free to use and works seamlessly across all supported chat apps. The voice recognition is powered by our AI engine, delivering high accuracy. You can switch between voice and typing anytime without losing your input flow. Would you like me to guide you through setting it up?"

Concise Style: "Yes — voice input in Chinese is fully supported and completely free. Just tap the mic icon to start. Need setup help?"

🎧 Scenario 3: Escalated Complaint

Context: An angry customer: "This is the THIRD time I've reported this bug. Your support is useless."

Professional Style: "I sincerely apologize for the repeated issue and the frustration it has caused. This is not the standard of service we want to provide, and I take full responsibility for the gaps in our previous responses. I've personally escalated this to our senior engineering team with priority status. Here's what will happen: (1) you'll receive a direct update from our lead engineer within 24 hours, (2) we'll provide a root cause analysis, and (3) I'll personally follow up to ensure resolution. Your ticket number is #CS-8721 for reference. Is there anything else I can do right now to make this right?"

Warm Style: "I hear you, and I'm truly sorry. You're right — this shouldn't have happened three times, and I understand why you're upset. I'm personally taking over your case and making sure this gets fixed properly. Our lead engineer will contact you directly within 24 hours with a plan. Your experience matters to us, and I won't stop until this is resolved. Thank you for your patience."

🎧 Scenario 4: Cancellation Request

Context: User writes: "Please cancel my account."

Professional Style: "I'm sorry to see you go! I've processed your cancellation request. Your data will be retained for 30 days in case you'd like to reactivate. Would you mind sharing what led to your decision? We value your feedback and it helps us improve."

Warm Style: "I'm sorry to hear you're leaving! We've processed your cancellation. If you ever want to come back, your account and data will be waiting for you for the next 30 days. We'd love to hear any feedback if you're open to sharing — it really helps us get better. Wishing you all the best! 🌟"

🎧 Scenario 5: Technical Support - Setup Issues

Context: A user who just installed Langou: "I installed the app but it's not showing up as my keyboard. What do I do?"

Professional Style: "Thank you for reaching out! This is a common first-time setup question. Here's how to enable Langou as your default keyboard: On Android, go to Settings → System → Languages & Input → On-screen Keyboard → Manage Keyboards, then toggle Langou on. Then open any chat app, long-press the space bar or globe icon, and select Langou from the keyboard switcher. If you encounter any specific error messages, please let me know and I'll help you troubleshoot further."

Concise Style: "Quick fix: Settings → System → Languages & Input → On-screen Keyboard → Manage Keyboards → toggle Langou ON. Then in any chat, long-press space/globe to switch to Langou. Let me know if it works!"
