🎨 Themes & Personalization

Make your keyboard truly yours

Express Yourself Through Your Keyboard

You stare at your keyboard thousands of times a day. It should feel like yours. Langou offers deep personalization options — from professionally designed themes to granular control over every visual and auditory element. Whether you want a cyberpunk neon aesthetic or a clean, distraction-free typing experience, Langou adapts to your taste.

🌃

Cyberpunk Theme

A futuristic aesthetic inspired by neon-lit cityscapes. Features vibrant electric blue (#00f0ff) as the primary accent, with deep charcoal backgrounds (#1a1a2e). Animated keypress glow effects give each tap a satisfying visual feedback. The candidate bar has a translucent glass-morphism effect. Perfect for night owls and tech enthusiasts who want their keyboard to feel like it belongs in a Blade Runner scene.

🌙

Dark Mode Theme

Pure elegance in darkness. True black (#000000) backgrounds with carefully chosen gray tones for key caps. Accent colors are desaturated to reduce eye strain during extended use. The Dark Mode theme reduces battery usage on OLED screens by up to 30% compared to light themes. Every element has been meticulously tuned for readability in low-light conditions — no harsh contrasts, no bleeding edges.

🤍

Minimal White Theme

Clean, bright, and professional. A crisp white background (#f8f9fa) with subtle gray borders and gentle shadows. Accent colors are warm but restrained — think Apple's design philosophy applied to a keyboard. The Minimal White theme is ideal for professional environments, bright offices, or anyone who prefers a clean and uncluttered interface. Focus is on readability and reducing visual fatigue.

🎚️

Customization Options

Beyond built-in themes, every aspect of the keyboard can be customized:

  • Font: Choose from 20+ fonts including system fonts, custom Google Fonts, and calligraphy-inspired options for Chinese characters
  • Key colors: Customize background, text, accent, and press-state colors for every key type
  • Key shapes: Rounded, squared, pill-shaped, or classic square keys
  • Key sounds: Mechanical keyboard clicks (Cherry MX Blue/Brown/Red), typewriter, soft tap, or silent mode
  • Key height: Adjust key elevation from completely flat to 3D raised keys
  • Keyboard height: Floating or docked mode, adjustable height slider
  • Background images: Set custom images or gradients as keyboard background
  • Animations: Tap ripple effects, smooth transitions, or static (battery-saving) mode
  • Sound feedback: Adjustable volume, haptic feedback intensity on supported phones
🎨

Theme Gallery & Sharing (Pro)

Pro users gain access to the Theme Gallery — a community-driven marketplace of user-created themes. Browse, try, and apply themes created by the Langou community. You can also share your own creations. Each theme in the gallery includes screenshots and a preview mode so you can see exactly what you're getting before applying.

📱

Platform-Specific Features

On Android, themes extend to the keyboard's edge-to-edge mode and navigation bar styling. The floating keyboard on Windows supports transparency effects (Windows 11's acrylic blur) and can be themed independently of your system theme. Both platforms sync theme preferences via Cloud Sync.
