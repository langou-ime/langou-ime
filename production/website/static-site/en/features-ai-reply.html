🤖 Multi-Style AI Reply Generation

Six distinct personalities, one keyboard

The AI Behind the Replies

Langou's reply engine is powered by Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5-Pro, a 420-billion parameter foundation model specifically fine-tuned for conversation generation. Unlike general-purpose chatbots that produce one-size-fits-all responses, Langou's model has been fine-tuned on millions of high-quality conversation pairs across six distinct communication styles. The result: replies that sound like you — or whoever you need to be in that moment.

💕

Sweet Style 💕

Designed for partners, crushes, and close friends. Sweet replies are warm, affectionate, and emotionally resonant. This style uses gentle language, loving nicknames, and heartwarming sentiments. Best for: romantic partners, close family, deepening a bond. See examples on our Sweet Style page.

😄

Humorous Style 😄

Witty, playful, and sometimes delightfully absurd. Humorous replies use puns, playful teasing, clever wordplay, and lighthearted references. This style excels at defusing tension, keeping conversations fun, and making people laugh. Best for: friends, group chats, breaking the ice. See examples on our Humorous Style page.

🤗

Warm Style 🤗

Empathetic, supportive, and caring. Warm replies validate the other person's feelings, offer comfort, and create a safe emotional space. This is the style to use when someone shares bad news, needs encouragement, or just needs to feel heard. Best for: supporting friends, consoling loved ones, empathetic responses. See examples on our Warm Style page.

💼

Professional Style 💼

Formal, respectful, and polished. Professional replies use proper grammar, appropriate vocabulary, and maintain a respectful distance. Perfect for workplace communication, client-facing messages, and any scenario where you need to sound competent and reliable. Best for: email, Slack with colleagues, client communication. See examples on our Professional Style page.

✂️

Concise Style ✂️

Short, direct, and efficient. Concise replies strip away every unnecessary word while preserving the core message. Perfect for busy professionals, quick confirmations, or when you just want to get to the point. Best for: quick replies, status updates, confirmations. See examples on our Concise Style page.

🗣️

Casual Style 🗣️

Natural, relaxed, and conversational. Casual replies sound like you're chatting with a friend over coffee. They use everyday language, contractions, and a natural rhythm. This is the default "me" style — authentic and unforced. Best for: everyday chats, close friends, relaxed settings. See examples on our Casual Style page.

⚙️

How Style Selection Works

The engine considers multiple factors when selecting reply candidates across all six styles:

  • Context-adaptive: Based on detected emotion and scenario, certain styles are weighted more heavily. An angry message automatically prioritizes Warm and Concise over Humorous.
  • Quick-switch: You can toggle between all six styles instantly with a single tap on the style selector.
  • Custom styles (Pro): Pro users can create custom style presets by mixing parameters — for example, "70% Professional + 30% Warm" for a boss who's also a mentor.

Try each style in real scenarios on our Use Cases page, or see the Context Awareness Engine that powers these intelligent suggestions.
